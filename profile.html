How this profile was created

The questions draw on established themes in later-life physical-health assessment: lower-limb strength and chair-rise ability, balance and falls screening, and functional activities such as reaching, changing position and floor transfers.

Each answer contributes 0, 1 or 2 points to one area. A higher number means your answers showed more reasons to explore that area. This is an original signposting profile, not a validated clinical outcome measure.

This profile provides general guidance only. It cannot diagnose a condition, estimate your falls risk or replace an individual assessment by an appropriately qualified health professional.
