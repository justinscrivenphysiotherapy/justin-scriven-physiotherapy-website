01 · Terms

Appointment terms

Bookings and payment

Appointments are provided by Justin Scriven Physiotherapy Ltd. A booking is accepted when it is confirmed through the online booking system or directly by the practice. Payment is due at the appointment unless it has been taken in advance during online booking.

Prices shown when booking or confirmed directly are the prices that apply to that appointment. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

Cancellations and missed appointments

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or rearrange. When less than 24 hours’ notice is given, or an appointment is missed, the full appointment fee will normally be charged because that time has been reserved for you and may not be available to another patient.

Please contact the practice as soon as possible if exceptional circumstances prevent you attending. Any decision not to apply the charge is at the practice’s reasonable discretion. If the practice has to cancel, you will be offered another appointment or a refund of any fee paid for that appointment.

Online bookings and cancellation rights

If you book remotely and ask for an appointment to take place within a statutory cancellation period, you are requesting that the service starts during that period. Once the appointment has been fully provided, any applicable right to cancel that completed service may be lost. If a service has started but has not been completed, you may be responsible for the proportion already supplied, subject always to your statutory rights.

Attending an appointment

Please provide information that is accurate and relevant to your care, and tell Justin about important changes in your symptoms, health or medication. Arriving late may shorten the available appointment time; the normal fee will still apply.

A parent or legal guardian must attend with every patient under 18. Consent to assessment or treatment can be withdrawn at any time, although payment remains due for services already provided.

Clinical decisions and outcomes

Assessment and treatment are tailored to the individual. Recommendations may change in response to findings, progress or new information. No particular outcome can be guaranteed. Where a service is not clinically appropriate, the practice may decline or stop treatment and, where suitable, recommend another source of care.

Information we may collect

  • Your name, date of birth and contact details.
  • Appointment, correspondence, billing and payment-status information.
  • Health information, clinical history, assessment findings, treatment notes, exercise plans and relevant reports.
  • Information supplied by a parent, guardian, referrer or healthcare professional where there is an appropriate reason and authority to share it.
  • Marketing preferences and interactions with Physio Life Skills emails.
  • Limited technical information generated when you use the website or embedded services, such as an IP address, browser, device and pages visited.

Health information is legally classed as special-category personal data and is given additional protection.

Why we use it

Information is used to respond to enquiries; arrange and administer appointments; assess, treat and support patients; keep appropriate clinical and financial records; communicate about care; process payments; meet professional, safeguarding, insurance, tax and legal duties; protect the practice and its systems; and improve services.

We use personal information where it is necessary to take steps at your request or provide our services under a contract, meet a legal obligation, or pursue legitimate interests such as safe administration and service improvement. We use consent where required, particularly for optional marketing and non-essential cookies. Health information is processed for the provision and management of healthcare by a regulated professional subject to confidentiality obligations, and on another lawful condition where required.

Systems and organisations we use

Information is shared only where necessary. Service providers may include:

  • Cliniko and PatientNotes for appointments, administration, clinical information and patient communication.
  • Zettle and PayPal for payments. The practice does not receive or store your complete card details.
  • Kit for Physio Life Skills emails where you have subscribed or where sending them is otherwise permitted.
  • Elfsight and Google when the Google Reviews feature is loaded.
  • Website hosting, security and technical-support providers.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, regulators, public authorities or another healthcare professional where necessary and lawful.

We do not sell personal information. Some technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, we rely on the provider’s appropriate UK data-transfer safeguards.

AI-assisted note-taking

The practice may use a secure AI-assisted note-taking tool to help prepare an accurate clinical record and allow more attention to remain on the consultation. Before it is used, you will be told what the tool does and how information is handled, and your consent will be requested. You may decline or ask for it to stop without this affecting your care. Justin remains responsible for reviewing and approving the final clinical record.

Any provider used for this purpose must be subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and data-processing arrangements. This notice will be updated with the provider and relevant processing details before routine use of the tool begins.

How long information is kept

Clinical records are retained in line with professional, legal and insurance requirements. Adult clinical records are normally kept for at least eight years after the end of treatment. Records relating to children are normally retained until the patient’s 25th birthday—or 26th birthday if treatment ended when they were 17—unless a longer period is required.

Financial records are normally retained for six years. Routine enquiries are generally kept for up to two years. Marketing information is kept until you unsubscribe or it is no longer needed. These periods may be extended where a complaint, claim, safeguarding concern or legal obligation requires it.

Keeping information safe

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, including access controls, password-protected systems, secure service providers and appropriate confidentiality procedures. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed as completely risk-free.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction, transfer, or object to certain uses. You may withdraw consent at any time where processing depends on consent. Some rights are limited where clinical or legal record-keeping duties apply.

To make a request or raise a concern, email office@justinscriven.co.uk. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

03 · Using the website

Website and exercise disclaimer

General information—not individual advice

The information, articles, videos and example exercises on this website are for general education. They do not replace an individual physiotherapy assessment, medical diagnosis or advice from a healthcare professional who understands your circumstances.

Exercises are not suitable for everyone. If you choose to try general information from the website, work within your own capabilities and stop if you develop significant pain, dizziness, chest pain, unusual breathlessness, weakness, numbness or feel unsafe. Seek appropriate professional advice when you are uncertain.

Urgent problems

This website and email address are not emergency services. Call 999 for a medical emergency. For urgent advice in England when it is not a life-threatening emergency, use NHS 111 online or call 111.

Accuracy, availability and external links

We aim to keep information accurate and useful, but healthcare knowledge and services change. Content may be updated without notice. We cannot guarantee that the website will always be available or free from technical errors.

Links and embedded content from other organisations are provided for convenience. Their content, availability and privacy practices are outside the control of Justin Scriven Physiotherapy Ltd.

Copyright

Unless stated otherwise, the original wording, design and materials on this website belong to Justin Scriven Physiotherapy Ltd. They may not be reproduced commercially without written permission. You may save or print reasonable extracts for your own personal use.

04 · Website data

Cookie information

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of information used by websites and online services. Some are essential for security and basic operation; others help measure use or support embedded features.

What this website uses

The site may use essential technologies needed to deliver pages securely. The embedded Elfsight Google Reviews feature and Google map may connect to Elfsight or Google and process technical information when they load. Links to Cliniko, PayPal, Apple Maps or other external services take you to those organisations, which apply their own cookie and privacy policies.

Google Analytics

We intend to use Google Analytics to understand, in broad terms, how people find and use the website. Analytics should not be activated until an appropriate consent control is in place. If enabled, non-essential analytics cookies will be set only after you choose to accept them, and you will be able to reject them without losing access to the main website.

Your choices

You can also restrict or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking essential technologies may affect parts of the website. This section will be updated if the services or cookies used by the site change.
