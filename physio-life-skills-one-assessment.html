Why Physio Life Skills?

I wanted to help before life got smaller.

For nearly 30 years I’ve helped people recover from pain and injury. Along the way, I’ve also seen how easy it is to stop taking the stairs, walking with friends or getting down to play with grandchildren, one small compromise at a time.

Sometimes that change feels like an inevitable part of ageing. But strength, balance and movement are things we can explore and work on. Physio Life Skills is my way of making that process understandable: find out where you are, choose what matters to you and build from there.

It’s about keeping your options open, not chasing a perfect score.

Three physical life skills

Three skills.
Working together.

01

Strength

The force to rise, lift, climb, carry and keep doing things for yourself.

02

Balance

Staying steady when you walk, turn or step onto uneven ground.

03

Movement

The freedom to reach, turn, kneel and get up from the floor.

We’ll explore each of these as part of your assessment. The emphasis will depend on your concerns, what we find and what you want to keep doing.

The Physio Life Skills approach

Measure what matters.
Then make it useful.

An assessment is only worthwhile if the results make sense and lead to something you can actually do.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Establish where you are now using meaningful, appropriate physical measures.

  2. 02

    Understand

    See what the results mean for your goals, confidence and everyday activities.

  3. 03

    Build

    Focus on a small number of priorities through a clear personal programme.

  4. 04

    Review

    Measure change, recognise progress and decide what would help you next.

Different people · Different priorities

Same starting point.
Room for what you need.

“Stairs are getting harder.”We might give strength more attention and look at how you manage steps and everyday movements.

“I want to get down to the floor.”We might focus on movement, together with the strength and steadiness that help you get back up.

Useful answers

Before you begin.

Is this ordinary physiotherapy?+

It is led by the same clinical reasoning, but the starting point is different. Physiotherapy often begins with pain or injury. Physio Life Skills begins with the physical qualities you want to understand, preserve or develop.

Do I need to be fit already?+

No. The assessment meets you where you are. It is not a competition and the purpose is not to compare you with the person beside you.

When will the assessment be available?+

Early-access appointments are planned for 2027 after the assessment has been carefully trialled. The appointment length and fee will be confirmed before booking opens. Join the priority list to hear when the first appointments become available.

Do I choose Strength, Balance or Movement?+

No. There is one Physio Life Skills assessment. We’ll look across all three and agree where to focus, guided by your goals and what we find.

What happens afterwards?+

We’ll agree practical next steps. You may feel ready to work on these yourself, or you may want support with exercises and a follow-up to review progress. If a concern needs more detailed assessment, we’ll discuss that too.
